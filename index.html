NIGHT DISTRICT · FACTORY OUTBREAK

DEAD SHIFT

First-person zombie survival in the factory, a free-roam night district outside — cars, a gun shop, a clinic and the Velvet Room club. One wallet, one arsenal.

18+ — Contains violence, horror themes and an adults-only club with costumed dancers (no nudity). You can reduce club content in Settings.
v1.0 · Three.js / WebGL2 · All sound synthesised in-browser

HOW TO PLAY

On foot

WASD move · Mouse look · Shift sprint · Space jump · Ctrl / C* crouch
Left click shoot · Right click aim (ADS) · R reload · 1–5 / wheel weapons · V melee
E interact · P phone · M map · Esc pause

In a car

E enter/exit at the driver's door · WASD drive · Space handbrake · C chase/hood camera · H horn · F flip a stuck car

The loop

Survive zombie waves in the factory to earn cash. Spend it at wall buys, the Gun Shop, the Clinic — or at the Velvet Room. Zombies drop health and ammo. Between waves you get 20 seconds to re-arm.

Phone & map

Your phone (P) has a working map with waypoints and GPS, messages, contacts that do real things (cab, ammo drop, mechanic, clinic, club booking), your bank ledger and a checklist.

* C toggles crouch on foot and camera in a car.
